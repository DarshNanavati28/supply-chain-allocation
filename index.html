Allocation rule
Deterministic · reviewable · same inputs, same result
1Sort by confirmed priority (high to low), then promised date (earliest first). Missing dates go last; order ID breaks ties.
2Allocate in that order. Fill the full request when stock allows.
3If short, allocate only when partial delivery is allowed and remaining stock meets the minimum. Otherwise defer the order.
4Each product has its own stock pool. Notes never affect allocation unless a person confirms a suggested priority.
